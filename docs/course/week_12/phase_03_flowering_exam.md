**Objective:** To verify the candidate's mastery of Phase III (Weeks 9-12) concepts: Card Anatomy, Sip Protocols, Multi-Agent Coordination, and Biological Logic.

**Q1:** Describe the "Three Sacred Methods" of a SDD Card. Why is high-fidelity reporting prioritized over boolean results? (15 Points)

**Q2:** Explain the "Irrigation Rule." What happens downstream when a Sip fails? (15 Points)

## Section 2: Coordination & Locks (30 Points)

**Q5:** How do agents in the Greenhouse communicate their progress without using natural language? (10 Points)

## Section 3: Biological Logic & Guards (40 Points)

**Q6:** Contrast Binary Logic with Gradient-Based Biological Logic. Give an example of a "health_index" fact. (15 Points)

**Q7:** What is a "Reasoning Guard"? Write a Green Syntax `GUARD` that checks if a code change justifies its impact on "Cognitive Load." (15 Points)

**Q8:** **Practical Design:** You are designing a "Self-Healing Trellis" for a server's memory management.
-   Identify the critical Fact to monitor.
-   Define the `health_index` threshold for triggering a `PRUNE`.
-   Describe the 3D visual feedback when the system "Self-Heals." (10 Points)
