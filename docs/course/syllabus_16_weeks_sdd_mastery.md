# 16-Week Syllabus: The Neural Greenhouse Masterclass (SDD Mastery)

**Architect:** Artsy Directora
**Focus:** Translating the Shifty Waters of the Mind into Immutable Structural Code.
**Environment:** The Greenhouse for Mental Health Development.

---

## Phase I: Seeding the Intent (Weeks 1-4)
*Goal: Deconstructing abstract psychological phenomena into raw spatial metaphors.*

### Week 1: The Failure of Intent
-   **Lecture:** Why AI agents hallucinate behavior.
-   **3D Vision:** Visualizing "Chaotic Noise" vs. "Structured Signal."
-   **Lab:** Identifying semantic gaps in traditional BDD.

### Week 2: The Neural Greenhouse Metaphor
-   **Lecture:** Code as a living organism. The Trellis vs. The Vine.
-   **3D Vision:** Designing the "Foundation Plate"—the spatial limit of a thought.
-   **Lab:** Mapping the `docs/` architecture as a root system.

### Week 3: Introduction to Green Syntax
-   **Lecture:** Grammar as a physical barrier.
-   **3D Vision:** Using Light and Color to represent grammar rules (Red = Violation, Green = Compliance).
-   **Lab:** Writing your first `SITUATION` block.

### Week 4: The Fact Registry
-   **Lecture:** The Soil of Truth. Immutable units of environmental reality.
-   **3D Vision:** Visualizing the Registry as a bioluminescent data-field.
-   **Lab:** Probing the environment for `python_version` and `os_type`.

## Phase II: Rooting the Restrictions (Weeks 5-8)
*Goal: Mastering the art of the Trellis—defining what is NOT allowed.*

### Week 5: Structural Restrictions I (Complexity)
-   **Lecture:** Pruning the Over-Engineered. Limits on loops and nesting.
-   **3D Vision:** Folds and Branching—visualizing cyclomatic complexity as organic density.
-   **Lab:** Implementing `max_depth` restrictions.

### Week 6: Structural Restrictions II (Tooling)
-   **Lecture:** The Allowed Instrument. Preventing tool hallucination.
-   **3D Vision:** The "Tool Rack"—spatial representations of available libraries.
-   **Lab:** Restricting AI to the `agent_utils` ecosystem.

### Week 7: The "No-Compile" Mandate
-   **Lecture:** Purity of the Source. Why we avoid the Opaque Binary.
-   **3D Vision:** Microstructures and Surface Detail—seeing logic without executing it.
-   **Lab:** Developing structural analysis scripts that bypass compilation.

### Week 8: Middle-Term Review: The Panic Room
-   **Assignment:** Design a "Panic Deceleration" Trellis.
-   **3D Vision:** Rhythmic motion and smooth gradients (Calm) vs. Sharp contrasts (Stress).
-   **Lab:** Week 8 Assessment: Certification of the "Cultivator" grade.

### Week 10: Sips and Irrigation

-   **3D Vision:** Flow Dynamics—visualizing data as a liquid passing through a filter.

### Week 11: Multi-Agent Coordination
-   **Lecture:** Many Gardeners, One Greenhouse. Preventing conflicts in the Fact Registry.
-   **3D Vision:** Swarm Intelligence—visualizing multiple agents as a neural network.
-   **Lab:** Coordinating two agents on a single `quanta_*` repository.

### Week 12: Advanced Green Syntax
-   **Lecture:** Logic Gates and Reasoning Guards.
-   **3D Vision:** Complex Folds and Biological Logic.
-   **Lab:** Implementing conditional restrictions based on the Fact Registry.
